# Research and tailoring reference

## Source hierarchy

1. Official employer job page and application form.
2. Official employer career, culture, program, and FAQ pages.
3. Current regulator or government records for objective authorization signals.
4. Large review aggregators for directional employee sentiment.
5. Individual reviews, forums, and social posts for hypotheses only.

Label employer statements as claims and employee reviews as anecdotal reports. A historical visa filing is evidence that an employer has sponsored someone; it is not proof that a specific role will receive sponsorship.

## Broad graduate programs

- Document program-wide requirements.
- Select no more functions than the application permits.
- Tailor to common requirements and the selected functions.
- Do not infer function-specific duties absent from official materials.
- Record selected functions in tailoring notes.

## Keyword selection

- High: repeated in official duties or eligibility and directly supported.
- Medium: appears once in official materials and is supported.
- Low: comes mainly from commentary or is only loosely supported.
- Exclude: unsupported, misleading, or tied to an ineligible function.

A strong bullet needs an action, context, and result. Keyword presence alone is not an improvement.
